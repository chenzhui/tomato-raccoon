# 欧美抗议社群冷启动与转化攻坚手册 (Community Launch Playbook)

> **核心原则**：抗议社区极度反感劣质垃圾推销，但极其热衷**高质量艺术原创与精神共鸣**。  
> **核心口号**：“Grow Tomatoes, Not Data Centers” (种番茄，不要数据中心)  
> **策略重点**：“艺术先驱，真诚共鸣，顺手带货”——先分享木刻版画与原创故事，吸引抗议同盟求购。

## 二、 病毒式 Reddit 帖子实操文案模板

### 帖子标题推荐 (Title Ideas)
* *Option A (草坪牌实拍图)*: "Made a yard sign for our neighborhood to cope with the relentless 60Hz hum outside. Grow tomatoes, not data centers."
* *Option B (木刻艺术分享)*: "Spent last weekend carving a linocut tribute for our fight in Loudoun County. Meet the Tomato Raccoon defender."
* *Option C (抗争共鸣)*: "Tired of hearing 'Data Center Alley is great for taxes' while our electric bills double. Here's a little design dedicated to our local farmland."

### 帖子正文模版 (Post Body - 真诚且不带直接硬广)
```text
Hey everyone,

Living in Northern Virginia over the past few years, it feels like every open field, wooded ridge, and peaceful meadow is being quietly rezoned for massive grey concrete boxes and roaring diesel generators. Between the constant low-frequency HVAC drone and the sky-rocketing utility bills, a few neighbors and I were talking about how much we miss when Virginia was known for fresh soil, tomatoes, and quiet nights.

I'm an independent printmaker/illustrator, and I carved this linocut print over the weekend: an angry little raccoon standing guard over his heirloom tomato vines against the backdrop of data center cooling towers ("Grow Tomatoes, Not Data Centers").

I printed a small batch of heavy cotton tees and weather-proof yard signs with a local US print shop for our community rally next week. If anyone in NOVA or Virginia wants one to put on their lawn or wear to the next zoning board hearing, drop a comment or check my bio. 

Keep protecting our soil and quiet nights. 🍅🦝
```

## 三、 评论区转化与互动 SOP (Conversion Flow)

1. **主帖绝对不要放直接购买链接**：
   - Reddit 规则严苛，主帖带外部电商链接极其容易被 AutoModerator 判定为垃圾广告封禁。
   - 链接应当放在你的 **Reddit 个人主页 Bio (Profile)** 处，并固定一条置顶帖介绍店铺。
2. **在热评第一条提供免费壁纸**：
   - 评论：“A lot of folks asked for a phone wallpaper version—here is a high-res clean link for anyone who wants to use it for free!”
   - 这种大度且利他的行为会瞬间赢得社区 90% 以上的好评赞同（Upvote）。
3. **当网友提问“Where can I get the yard sign / shirt?”时**：
   - 礼貌自然地回复：“Thank you so much! You can find the heavy yard signs and cotton tees on our small shop here: [tomatoraccoon.com/products/yard-sign](https://tomatoraccoon.com) (use code `NOVA10` for 10% off our community neighbor discount).”
