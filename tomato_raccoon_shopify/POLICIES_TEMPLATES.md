> **使用说明**：在 Shopify 后台打开 **Settings -> Policies**，将对应模块的内容直接复制粘贴进各输入框，点击 **Save** 即可。内容已完全适配 POD 定制、美国本地印刷与 Airwallex 国际收单审核标准。

## 1. Shipping Policy (运输政策)

```text
SHIPPING & PRODUCTION POLICY

Thank you for supporting Tomato Raccoon and the independent grass-roots movement. All of our protest apparel, yard signs, and decals are printed on-demand with our vetted print partners right here in the United States.

1. Production Time
Every item is made especially for you as soon as you place an order. 
- Apparel (T-shirts & Hoodies): 2–4 business days
- Yard Signs & Decals: 1–3 business days

2. Shipping Methods & Delivery Times
We ship across the contiguous United States, Hawaii, Alaska, and APO/FPO addresses via USPS (United States Postal Service).
- USPS Ground Advantage: 3–5 business days after fulfillment
- Priority Mail: 2–3 business days after fulfillment
*Standard shipping across the United States is FREE on all orders over $50.*

3. Order Tracking
Once your package is inspected, packed, and scanned by USPS, you will immediately receive an automated shipping confirmation email containing your official USPS tracking number and tracking link. Please allow 24 hours for the tracking page to update.

4. Lost or Stolen Packages
If your tracking status shows "Delivered" but you have not received your item, please check around your porch, with neighbors, or your local postal carrier. If the package remains missing after 48 hours, reach out to us at support@tomatoraccoon.com and we will assist in resolving the case.
```

## 2. Refund & Return Policy (退换货政策)

```text
REFUND & RETURN POLICY

At Tomato Raccoon, every piece of protest gear is custom-printed on demand to reduce textile waste and support local US printing facilities. Because each item is produced uniquely upon your order, please review our policy below:

1. Defective, Damaged, or Misprinted Items (100% Free Replacement or Refund)
We stand behind the craft of our garments and materials. If your order arrives with:
- A manufacturing defect, torn fabric, or damaged yard sign/stand;
- Visible printing errors or misaligned artwork;
- An incorrect item, size, or color sent by mistake;

Please email support@tomatoraccoon.com within 30 days of delivery. Include:
1. Your order number (e.g., #TR-1024)
2. A clear photograph displaying the defect or incorrect item.
We will rush a brand-new reprint to your door at absolutely zero additional cost, or issue a full refund to your original payment method. You do not need to ship the defective item back.

2. Sizing & Change of Mind
Because items are printed on demand, we cannot accept general returns or issue refunds for ordering the incorrect size or changing your mind once production begins. Please consult our detailed Size Guides on each product page before finalizing your purchase.

3. Cancellations
Orders enter our automated US production pipeline quickly. If you need to modify or cancel an order, email support@tomatoraccoon.com within 2 hours of placing the order. Once the order status moves to "In Production", it cannot be modified or canceled.
```

Tomato Raccoon Studio values your privacy and data security. This Privacy Policy describes how your personal information is collected, used, and shared when you visit or make a purchase from our store.

1. Personal Information We Collect
When you purchase or attempt to purchase an item, we collect certain information from you, including your name, billing address, shipping address, payment information (processed securely through certified gateways, we never store full credit card details on our servers), email address, and phone number.

We use your Order Information to:
- Fulfill and route orders through our US print-on-demand fulfillment centers (Printify);
- Communicate with you regarding tracking and customer service inquiries;

3. Sharing Your Information
We share your information solely with essential third parties to help us run our store:
- Shopify powers our online storefront and checkout security;

We will never sell, rent, or trade your personal data to external advertisers.

4. Your Rights
If you are a resident of California or regions with specific privacy statutes, you have the right to access personal information we hold about you and to ask that your personal information be corrected, updated, or deleted. Contact us at support@tomatoraccoon.com.
